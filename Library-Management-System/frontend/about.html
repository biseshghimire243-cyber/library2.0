About Library Management System

📚 Project Overview

The Library Management System is a web-based application developed to simplify the management of books, students, and library transactions. It provides an easy way for librarians to manage the entire library from a single dashboard.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify library operations through a modern, reliable, and user-friendly digital management system. We aim to help librarians efficiently organize books, manage students, and streamline borrowing activities.

Our Vision

We envision a smart library where technology improves learning, enhances accessibility, and creates a seamless experience for both students and librarians.
